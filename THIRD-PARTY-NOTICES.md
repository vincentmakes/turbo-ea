## SheetJS Community Edition (`xlsx`)

- **Component:** Excel/CSV import and export in the inventory. The `xlsx` library is
  vendored into the repository as a tarball.
- **Location:** `frontend/xlsx-0.20.3.tgz` (SheetJS Community Edition, v0.20.3).
- **Project:** [SheetJS](https://sheetjs.com/) — https://git.sheetjs.com/sheetjs/sheetjs
- **Original license:** Apache License 2.0

## Simple Icons

- **Component:** The built-in brand-icon pack a card logo can be set from. The icons
  are rasterised from Simple Icons' SVG sources at generation time and the resulting
  PNGs are committed to this repository and shipped inside the backend image.
- **Location:** `backend/app/services/data/brand_icons.pack` + `brand_icons.json`
  (generated by `frontend/scripts/gen-brand-icons.mjs` from `simple-icons` v16).
- **Project:** [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) — https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- **Original license:** CC0 1.0 Universal

---

## SVG Logos (gilbarbara)

- **Component:** The full-colour half of the same bundled brand-icon pack, offered
  as a card logo. Rasterised from the SVG sources at generation time and shipped
  inside the backend image alongside the Simple Icons set.
- **Location:** `backend/app/services/data/brand_icons.pack` + `brand_icons.json`
  (generated by `frontend/scripts/gen-brand-icons.mjs` from `@iconify-json/logos`,
  which packages https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos).
- **Project:** [SVG Logos](https://svgporn.com/) by Gil Barbara — https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos
- **Original license:** CC0 1.0 Universal

Brand names, logos and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
The CC0 dedications above cover the icon artwork, not the underlying trademarks.

## Material Symbols (Google)

- **Component:** Icon path geometry. Used three ways: the card-type glyph baked
  into DrawIO card shapes, the icon picker, and the NexaTech demo house marks —
  the last of which are rasterised from this geometry and committed as PNGs.
- **Location:** `frontend/src/features/diagrams/iconPaths.ts` (generated by
  `frontend/scripts/gen-diagram-icon-paths.mjs` from `@material-symbols/svg-400`,
  with `@mui/icons-material` covering a handful of legacy names) and
  `backend/app/services/data/demo_logos/*.png` (generated by
  `frontend/scripts/gen-demo-logos.mjs`).
- **Project:** [Material Symbols](https://fonts.google.com/icons) — https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
- **Original license:** Apache License 2.0

## draw.io / diagrams.net (jgraph)

- **Component:** The embedded diagram editor. The frontend Docker image clones and
  bundles draw.io at build time and serves it under `/drawio/`.
- **Version:** jgraph/drawio v31.1.8 — https://github.com/jgraph/drawio
- **Original license:** Apache License 2.0
